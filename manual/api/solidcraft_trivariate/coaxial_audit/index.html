Module coaxial_audit

Independent audit of ordered coaxial cut/add programs.

This module reconstructs the occupancy history from the recipe and compares the saved integer nets with independently derived circular and square charts. It never calls the geometry constructor.

The accepted template has a square core of half-width r_min/4, concentric rational circular bands with strictly increasing integer radii, and an outer square at half-width 10 mm. Since sqrt(2) r_min/4 < r_min <= 8 < 10, these boundaries are strictly nested. Each angular chart is one of the eight monotone half-quadrant arcs; radial charts join consecutive nested boundaries. Exact whole-face matching checks their seams, and the planar Bernstein certificate checks a strictly positive Jacobian on every chart. Axial slabs are disjoint open intervals whose ends form the full sorted partition. Together these facts establish coverage and injectivity for this fixed coaxial template, rather than for arbitrary trivariate nets. The volume formula sums exact disk/annulus/square areas over the audited cells; f64 is used only to report multiples of pi.
