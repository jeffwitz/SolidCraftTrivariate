What the audit establishes

Exact interval logic, explicit limits

Exact selection

The recipe gives integer-millimetre axial intervals. Each open interval is selected by the requested operation; boundary caps retain their source operand and orientation.

Topology and measure

The audit checks the canonical model, selected intervals, cell incidence, component count and volume against the analytic section area.

Declared scope

This axial constructor accepts two operands with the same supported section and axis. It does not claim a general 3D CSG engine or export these axial models to STEP.
