Strict coefficient audit and continuous bounds for the fixed annular pair.

The oracle reconstructs the stored binary64 controls independently of the builder. Its continuous claims apply to the real rational map defined by those stored values over the complete closed parameter cubes.

Let q=M/2^53 be the stored middle weight, s=1-u, and t=u. The planar map has homogeneous denominator D=s²+2qst+t² and numerators X=s²+2qst, Y=t²+2qst, scaled by radius 2-v. Bernstein convexity gives q<=D<=1. Direct expansion gives X²+Y²-D²=(4q²-2)s²t² and XY'-YX'=2q(s²+t²)+2st>=q. Thus its planar determinant is at least q. The stored q is checked exactly from its binary mantissa: q>7/10 and |q²-1/2|<2^-51, the latter following from the u128 comparison |M²-2^105|<2^55. Rationalizing the radius error then gives 2|4q²-2|/(16q²)<2^-50 mm.

At every integer axial level n<=2000, the exact product q*n and its rounded binary64 value, expressed in units of 2^-53, differ by at most 2^10; the audit checks this integer inequality for all 2001 levels using u128 arithmetic. Therefore each rounded product error is at most 2^-43. Since D>=q>1/2 and 2st<=1/2, the plane elevation error is below 2^-43 mm and the axial derivative is greater than h-2^-42. For every allowed h>=1, this is greater than 3h/4. The full determinant exceeds (7/10)*(3h/4)=21h/40>h/2; min_height/2 is consequently a conservative lower bound in mm³. The angular coordinate is strictly increasing, the radius decreases with v, and elevation increases with w; this module reports local continuous bounds and does not certify other families.
