How the annulus qualification is scoped

The ten-volume construction starts from a prepared five-patch disk section and two rational half-turns. Exact-fraction checks recover all four meridional profiles and their joins. The fillet circle identity holds across both complete spans per corner; the independent volume oracle compares quadrature against analytic or STEP references. Positive determinant, oriented joins and a degree-one section boundary establish the stated cover for these models.

The lower-bound argument applies only to regular, conforming quadrangulations of a disk section with regular corners and to injective product sweeps. It proves ten volumes minimal in that stated construction class; it is not a minimum over all possible 3D hexahedral partitions. IGA degrees of freedom and mechanical performance for this ten-volume family remain unmeasured.
