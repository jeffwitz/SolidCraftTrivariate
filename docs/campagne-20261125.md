## How the campaign ran

Before each batch, the coordinator recorded its base revision, intended inputs, independent oracle, acceptance criteria and resource budget. Separate agents owned construction and verification. Integration required the CLI and `make check` to pass on the candidate revision. A rejection on a required case remained in the coverage denominator. Failed attempts and diagnostic reruns were kept even when a later method succeeded. The last ten minutes were reserved for final checks and publication.

## 23:23–23:41 UTC · Curved blocks and interfaces

C1–C4 built two quarter-annular blocks, matched their shared face, certified the stored maps and subdivided them in homogeneous coordinates. The test family used radii of 1 and 2 mm and positive integer heights up to 1,000 mm per block. Trace comparison exercised all eight orientations of a quadrilateral. The certificate covered the closed parameter cube and the actual stored binary coefficients. Refinement transferred a physical affine field in the same rational basis.

At 23:31 the two-block example had one interface and ten exterior sides. For heights of 1 and 2 mm, the determinant exceeded 0.7071 mm³. The certified radial and axial errors were at most 6.84 × 10⁻¹⁷ and 1.58 × 10⁻¹⁶ mm. Negative tests included a one-ULP perturbation, an endpoint cut and an incorrectly weighted field transfer. [Annular certificate](annular-certificate.md)

E1–E7 developed the geometry for a filleted L-bracket. The pierced square used four sectors. The inner R6 corner used two 45° spans. Exact trace elevation made adjacent sections compatible. The planar certificate evaluated the Bernstein coefficients of the rational Jacobian numerator and bounded its denominator. The bracket audit independently checked the expected tile inventory, frames, extrusions, circular walls and face names. [Planar certificate](planar-certificate.md)
