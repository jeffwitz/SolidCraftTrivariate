Verification and evidence scope

Verification asks whether the implemented equations and algorithms are solved as intended. It is distinct from validation against physical observations. This project combines exact or interval-based geometric audits, independent analytic oracles, manufactured-solution mechanics, mesh-convergence studies, and comparisons with independent geometry or finite-element software. Each method answers a different question; no single pass should be generalized beyond its declared domain and discretization.

Geometric and algebraic checks

Native Rust constructors and audits live beside one another but are separate implementations for each family (for example, src/curved_sketch.rs and src/curved_sketch_audit.rs). Family audits check declared geometry invariants such as coverage, exact volume, interfaces, and a named Jacobian certificate. The Python references in tests/references provide independent oracles for selected results. A sampled grid is a diagnostic only; a continuous certificate must name its domain, method, and bounds. If a proof or interval subdivision reaches its budget without a conclusion, the result is indeterminate rather than accepted.

STEP exchange and readback

The exporter in src/step_boundary.rs serializes the audited boundary using STEP AP214. ISO 10303-21 specifies the clear-text exchange encoding, while ISO 10303-42 defines generic geometric and topological representation resources; neither standard makes a producer’s export self-validating. The project gate therefore distinguishes serialization from a readback in another CAD kernel. Even agreement of volume, area, and moments is not a complete shape-identity test: Kuzminykh and Hoffmann construct noncongruent solids sharing such invariants. Readback checks should include closure/validity and classifications at selected material, void, and boundary points, with reader tolerances stated. See ISO 10303-21:2016, ISO 10303-42:2025, and A. Kuzminykh and C. Hoffmann, “On validating STEP product data exchange,” Computer-Aided Design 40(2) (2008), 133–138, DOI 10.1016/j.cad.2007.09.004.

Preserve counterexamples and regressions under tests/counterexamples and tests/regressions. An invalid input that is required to be rejected is part of coverage, and rejection must leave the prior model or published artifact unchanged. Rebuild tracked geometry from its source recipe when the partition changes, then run:

make check-published-geometry

Mechanics and the Method of Manufactured Solutions

The Method of Manufactured Solutions (MMS) chooses a smooth displacement field, applies the continuum elasticity operator analytically to derive the body force, and prescribes exact boundary data. The numerical solution can then be compared with the chosen field in displacement and energy norms. MMS is a code-verification technique: a successful rate study checks the coded operator, loads, boundary conditions, quadrature, and solver together for that case. It does not establish accuracy for arbitrary loads or validate a physical material model.

The native T-junction example examples/manufactured_t.rs is a concrete regression of a two-rate Q2 mesh. It uses a nonzero affine displacement plus a smooth trigonometric perturbation on [0,2] × [0,2] × [0,1]; the right half is refined twice as finely in y, and the coarse interface is restricted exactly onto two fine slave faces. The recipe and acceptance bands are recorded in docs/campaign-run-ledger.md under M1. In particular, it requires an affine patch test, a reduced residual check, observed L2 and H1 rates, and a quadrature comparison. This is evidence for that mesh family and manufactured field, not universal convergence.

For the S2 variable-size extracted-space roadmap study, use the regular affine cube and its preregistered refinement levels and error norms in docs/recipe-driven-tsplines.md. Do not substitute a singular geometry and retain the smooth-case rate thresholds: the roadmap explicitly excludes that inference.

Singular fillet caveat

The four-sector annulus fillet has analytically identified Jacobian zeros on four circles (at the two end planes and the stated radii). The proof of volume and quadrant coverage does not make that map locally regular at those sets. Consequently, results for the singular fillet are experimental evidence for the declared parameterization and oracle, not qualification as a regular IGA chart. See docs/annulus-original-edge-states.md and docs/results/inherited-continuity/README.md. Do not use good volume agreement to erase the singularity or imply mechanics qualification there.

Independent mechanics comparisons

Comparisons among native trivariate, tetrahedral, or immersed spline solves are meaningful only when the physical domain, boundary conditions, material, loads, and observables are aligned. Report those definitions along with mesh or basis size, quadrature, solver tolerance, residual, norm definition, and resource limits. Different parameterizations can have different discrete spaces even when their audited solid is the same. A field discrepancy is therefore a discretization comparison, not necessarily a geometry defect. The repository’s campaign protocol and machine-readable measurements are linked from docs/campaign-run-ledger.md; preserve failed, timed-out, and indeterminate trials in the denominator and state their limits.

As one current mechanics comparison, the crossed-tool bending study reports 0.305584 mm for the audited rational IGA model and 0.306089 mm for a matched HEX20 mesh, both with 11,112 free vector degrees of freedom; their displacement difference is 0.165% of the HEX20 value. A finer HEX20 reference with 125,004 free vector degrees of freedom gives 0.307772 mm. The study explicitly qualifies global compliance for this geometry and loading, not local stress accuracy or runtime. Reproduce the selected runs with make check-crossed-bending and see the measurement report.

Reproducible project gates from the repository root are:

cargo test --locked --offline
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
make check

make check runs the Rust and Python suites, demonstrations, and published geometry checks. Specialized mechanics studies have separate targets such as make check-local-thb and make check-cylinder-iga; these should be reported by name rather than implied by the general gate.

Primary verification reference

For the relation between a manufactured load and the exact field it prescribes, see [Roache2009].

[Roache2009]

P. J. Roache, “Fundamentals of Verification and Validation,” Hermes, 2009, Chapter 3: “Method of Manufactured Solutions.” Author’s manuscript. MMS derives exact source terms from an assumed field and is used to check numerical implementation and observed order; it is not physical validation.
