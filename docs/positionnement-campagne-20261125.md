# Feature-Based Volumetric Construction and Isogeometric Analysis

This campaign asks which recipes let an operator provide untrimmed maps, an audit over the closed domain, a usable displacement space, and an update whose cost can be measured at fixed accuracy. Feature-guided volumetric construction, strong continuity between nested NURBS traces, and sensitivity to design dimensions all have published precedents.

| Question | Published result | Reproducible campaign result |
|---|---|---|
| Construction from features | Chen, Bu, Jin, Xu, and Li organize volume construction into segmentation, subdomain parameterization, and merging. Their paper presents complex 3D models. [Chen et al., 2022](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0010448522000252) | All 1,681 permitted radius pairs for the bracket's two-hole family were constructed and audited. Networks, incidences, and selections carry verifiable identities. The grid covers both radii in sixteenths of a millimeter. [R15](results/bracket-radius-grid-20261125.json) |
| Design dimensions and mechanical objectives | Chen, Zhang, Wu, Xu, and Li link dimensions, intermediate parameters, subvolumes, and stiffness or stress objectives. They derive sensitivity equations and describe four optimization groups. [Chen et al., 2024](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S001044852300204X) | For the bracket radius, the discrete tangent and adjoint give a compliance derivative of 1.6013 N·mm/mm at h0/q8. The three Taylor-remainder ratios are about four when the step is halved. The two observables in this case are related by traction work. [R14](results/bracket-radius-sensitivity-20261125.json) |
| Trace continuity and refinement | Kleiss, Pechstein, Jüttler, and Tomar address conforming interfaces and hanging nodes, with fine NURBS weights derived from coarse refinement. Their IETI formulation covers subdomain decomposition, coupling, and preconditioning. [Author manuscript, §§ 3.2 and 5.1](https://www.ag.jku.at/pubs/2012kpjt.pdf) | Export X2 provides the rational constraints for a 3D bracket. Independent reconstruction finds rank 178 for the trace equations and six rigid modes in both cases with two local levels. Field agreement with the native solver is measured at every degree of freedom. [X2](results/adaptive-package-20261125.json) |
| Construction of a valid interior map | Hinz and Buffa use elliptic formulations to parameterize planar multipatch domains from their boundaries. Their harmonic analysis uses a convex parametric domain and a boundary correspondence. [Hinz and Buffa, 2024](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00366-024-01997-x) | G13 starts from a section that has already been audited. At each height, the planar transformation is a similarity whose complex scale factor traverses a line segment. The exact minimum of its squared modulus, followed by the height, gives a determinant bound and an explicit inverse. The family covers two sections and rejects pinches. [G13](results/ruled-sweep-20261125.json) |
| Geometric continuity and validity | Wang, Chen, and Zhang describe iMSO generation and junction adjustment to obtain G¹ geometric continuity. Their discussion of examples reports negative Jacobians that remain after adjustment. [Wang et al., 2026, discussion and conclusion](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s42492-026-00217-1) | V1b provides a local Bernstein bound, an inversion witness, or an indeterminate result when the budget is exhausted. For the bracket, the partition proof and mechanical H¹ assembly also use the family's incidences and topology. [V1b](results/spatial-certificate-20261125.json) |

The two Chen papers were consulted through the publishers' previews. Their detailed acceptance criteria, update costs, and rejection protocols remain to be checked in the full texts. Campaign timing comparisons cover the implementations actually run, with their own geometries and error budgets. They are not benchmarks of the software from the five cited teams.
