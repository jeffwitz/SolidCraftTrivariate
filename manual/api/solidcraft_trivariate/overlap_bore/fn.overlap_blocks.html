Expand description

Build the fixed two-overlapping-circle bore as eight rational sectors.

The right half uses increasing conic parameter from the lower intersection to the upper intersection. The left half is its direct pi rotation. For each half, sector index 00..03 follows the outer polyline from the lower square seam, across the bottom/right/top edges, to the upper square seam. Controls are flattened in (angle, radial, z) order, with z fastest; radial index 0 is the square-side curve and radial index 1 the bore arc.

The approximation S_NUM/S_DEN substitutes for sqrt(3); construction of the represented rational model is exact, while the tiny boundary mismatch to the ideal intersection is certified independently by the audit.
