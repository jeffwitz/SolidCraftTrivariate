FieldCraft analysis exchange

The desktop bridge connects a SolidCraft command plugin to the native Rust mechanical solver and to FieldCraft’s results viewer. The plugin’s WebAssembly component can call declared SolidCraft commands and exchange files in the approved companion directory. The desktop launcher runs the native processes.

Domain and load

A solve takes an explicit trivariate recipe and a mechanical job. The job selects support and traction surfaces using feature provenance and, where specified, a plane. An imported STEP boundary alone has no trivariate basis. A recipe solve concerns the named model even when a different CAD document is active. After a host-only Boolean, fillet or chamfer, the displayed body can differ from the last complete trivariate model.

The reference bracket has ten blocks and eleven internal interfaces. The sharp stock is clamped on the outer profile at \(y=0\) and loaded on the outer profile at \(y=50\) mm. A uniform traction of 6.25 MPa points in positive \(x\). The linear elastic material has \(E=210000\) MPa and \(\\nu=0.3\). With the Q2 field, order-eight Gauss integration and no refinement, the run has 171 scalar unknowns before essential constraints, a 2000 N applied resultant, a support reaction of approximately −2000 N in \(x\), and 135.44475 N mm of strain energy. These values identify this discretization. See Adaptive solves, pressure and design derivatives for the mechanical formulation.

Each sampled hexahedron is a display element within a volume chart. Samples on opposite sides of an interface can have different one-sided stresses. The displacement vector and stress fields are evaluated on the solved charts. A stress maximum on this plotting grid is a sampled maximum.

Validation

A replay should check three separate results: the solver’s residual and reaction balance, agreement of the sampled fields after a FieldCraft read/export round trip, and a contour drawn by the released FieldCraft CLI. Each catches a different failure, including wrong unit conversion or loss of the solved result during archive loading. See the FieldCraft 0.8.1 results codec and the SolidCraft plugin build.

On the reference bracket at four display subdivisions per chart direction, the released FieldCraft reader returned all 1,250 chart samples in source order. Comparing its ASCII VTU with the native VTU gave maximum absolute differences of \(1.4\times10^{-17}\) mm for displacement and \(2.9\times10^{-14}\) MPa over all six stress components. The round-trip check explicitly permutes the six symmetric stress entries into VTK order (xx, yy, zz, xy, yz, xz). A scalar von Mises comparison alone cannot catch every axis swap.
