# Inherited extracted elasticity qualification protocol

## Scope and baseline

This protocol qualifies the variable-size extracted-element assembler and the inherited rational field on the tall circular cylinder. The baseline is published revision `3f9d2d915c918121503668691b489a39781c6dd5`. The isolated checkout used for the experiments had an identical tracked tree. The implementation and its controls are in `src/extracted_space.rs`, `tests/extracted_space.rs`, and `tests/references/inherited_cylinder_torsion.job.json`.

The source geometry is `docs/site/box-cylinder/tall/cylinder.model.json`. Its independent mechanical reference is `docs/site/box-cylinder/tall/cylinder-torsion.json`, generated by `tests/references/cylinder_torsion.py`. The target material is 2.6 MPa, Poisson ratio 0.3, giving shear modulus 1 MPa. The cylinder has radius 5 mm, height 36 mm, center `(20,20)` mm and twist `0.001 mm^-1`.

## Numerical protocol

1. Reproduce the legacy degree-preserving C0 case at axial degree one: 204 vector coefficients, quadrature order 16, fixed lower end, and analytic top torsion traction.
2. Check one Q2 affine element against the existing Q2 assembler at the same quadrature order. Relative entrywise stiffness discrepancy must be at most `2e-11`.
3. At axial degree two, compare C0 and C1 spaces on identical volume quadrature. Verify `K_C1 = L^T K_C0 L` and `f_C1 = L^T f_C0` with sparse coefficient transfer `L`; each relative defect must be at most `3e-11`.
4. Solve the analytic torsion case. Applied torque and strain energy must agree with the independent reference to relative `2e-10`; support and applied torque must balance to `2e-10`; the constrained solve residual must remain below `1e-9`.
5. Measure the non-affine axial manufactured field `u_z=sin(pi*(z-z_min)/36) mm`, with analytic body force and analytic traction on every declared design face. Compare `(p,C)=(1,0),(2,1),(3,2)` and same-degree C0 controls. Record scalar/vector DOFs, nonzeros, quadrature work, L2 displacement error, energy error, solve residual, and iterations. The preregistered acceptance is decreasing energy error along the p1-C0, p2-C1, p3-C2 sequence. No requirement states that increasing continuity at fixed degree must decrease error; report its error/DOF trade-off.

All global stiffness storage remains block CSR. Element-local matrices may be dense over the variable local support. Geometry evaluation and extracted field evaluation share the quadrature point but retain separate contracts.

## Refusals and limits

Reject unsupported polynomial degree, malformed sparse extraction, nonpositive rational weights or Jacobians, duplicate local global IDs, inconsistent local IDs across quadrature points, invalid material data, unresolved end constraints, and unknown mechanical case. A refusal must occur before any output replacement.

The torsion oracle applies only to the circular reference. The manufactured axial test measures one smooth physical solution and does not establish convergence for arbitrary curved boundaries, singular solutions, or locally refined spaces. Nonlinear P-spline cases and local refinement are outside this lot.

## Blind-pocket extension

The real-piece extension uses `docs/site/data/plate_blind_pocket/plate_blind_pocket.model.json` and `tests/references/inherited_blind_pocket_mms.job.json`. It uses the same sine axial displacement over the full physical z range, the matching body force, and the exact Cauchy traction on every face returned by the independent boundary incidence enumeration. This includes the pocket floor and the exposed step faces. The bottom exterior face is clamped. Compare `(p,C)=(1,0),(2,1),(3,2)`; require strictly increasing scalar DOFs and strictly decreasing L2 displacement and energy errors. Record assembly, solve, and error-evaluation time separately. Timings are descriptive and have no pass threshold. Same-degree C0 rows are diagnostic controls; no claim requires C1 to improve error at fixed p.

The first blind-pocket run was a pilot before this extension was added. It is not counted as qualification; rerun the named test after this protocol update.

The blind-pocket energy and displacement errors decrease at each measured degree/continuity step. C1 at fixed degree reduces the DOF count but is not asserted to beat C0 error at that same degree. ## Pilot and qualification separation

Initial implementation checks were exploratory. They are retained as debugging evidence and are not counted as qualification. The recorded acceptance data comes from rerunning the named tests after this protocol was added.
