Which model does the solver see?

A face extrusion on the bracket illustrates the importance of identifying the operand. The Rust kernel can construct the spline extrusion tool. SolidCraft can combine its STEP boundary with an edited bracket and display the resulting feature. The tool still describes only the added or removed volume. An elasticity solve on that tool would impose its supports and loads on a different body from the one in the viewport.

The geometry path

A native trivariate model contains rational maps, their interfaces and boundary identifiers. The STEP boundary supplies SolidCraft’s renderer with faces it can tessellate. It does not carry the interior map network. A native body edit can therefore change the CAD boundary without producing a new trivariate analysis domain. Preserve the recipe, model and their lineage alongside the SolidCraft design.

The analysis path

A mechanical job ties its material, support and load to a particular volume model and named boundary faces. The Rust solver assembles a displacement field on those volume maps. A separate visualization samples this field for display. Increasing the density of display samples smooths the picture. Changing the analysis space or its quadrature changes the numerical experiment.

This distinction matters at a constrained edge. Two display elements can share the same displacement sample while the one-sided stress differs. Interpret displayed nodal values using the field definitions in FieldCraft analysis exchange. To change the physics, edit and rerun the mechanical job and retain the result and job together.
