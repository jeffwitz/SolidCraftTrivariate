Explanation

  • Which model does the solver see?

Why count cells, supports and unknowns separately?

Imagine drilling a circular hole through a plate. Several rational maps can surround the hole, each with a nonsingular parameterization. Two neighboring cells can share a complete face and still have different transverse derivatives there. Their traces suffice to join continuous displacement values. Matching physical gradients requires more information about both maps.

A NURBS patch can group several compatible Bézier spans. With a knot of multiplicity equal to its degree, the field can remain C⁰ across the knot. This grouping reduces the number of design objects even when assembly still integrates exactly the same spans. Recovering the original knot vector can change the field dimension. Removing a redundant geometry knot is a separate operation with a coefficient-level re-insertion test.

Consider the S3 plate. Of its 191 full faces, 62 pairs reconstruct a single Bézier parent. A weaker first-derivative test admits 73 interfaces for the selected C¹ field. The free field dimension drops from 2,679 to 1,710 on the recorded test, while relative energy-norm error changes from 3.6405% to 3.6417%. This measurement explains why the library exposes the choice of field. A different load can reveal a much larger loss of accuracy. S3 measurements and counterexamples

Why retain a source face after refinement?

A traction is prescribed on a face of the designed part. Refining a field may split its integration domain into many leaf faces. Without the mapping back to the named face, a solver could integrate a different load after each edit. The strip package saves the recipe-derived model and refinement plan. Replaying the package reconstructs each exterior leaf’s source selection before either a clamp or a traction is applied.

The reference chapters describe the separate tests for geometric validity, basis nesting and mechanical error.

Why a block count cannot select a partition by itself

A separator between two polygonal holes determines the vertices available to both surrounding quad rings. A wide separation margin can still place one vertex almost on the extension of a hole edge. That cell becomes nearly flat at its corner. For a planar Jacobian with singular values \(s_1\geq s_2>0\), the condition number \(s_1/s_2\) measures directional distortion. Sampling it in 3D on a thin extrusion can chiefly measure thickness. Report the planar value, corner angles and certified volume determinant alongside the number of cells and the sample grid.

The native polygonal planner compares a bounded set of construction candidates. A low count is an optimum only when a lower bound is reached under the same interface and layering rules. Hinz and Buffa investigate a complementary question: given the boundary contours and a parametric multipatch domain, how can an elliptic PDE produce a valid interior map? That approach provides a comparison for map quality once topology has been chosen. Hinz and Buffa (2024).
