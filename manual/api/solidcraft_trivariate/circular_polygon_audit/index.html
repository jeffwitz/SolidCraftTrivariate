Module circular_polygon_audit

Expand description

Independent continuous audit for polygonal prisms with exact circular bores.

The exterior material is audited as a G18 polygon with disjoint guard diamonds removed. Each guard diamond is then filled by eight rational sectors. Exact coefficient reconstruction proves the circle identity and the half-interval de Casteljau restrictions. Outward-rounded Bernstein bounds prove a positive planar Jacobian on every closed sector.

The guard diamond strictly contains its circle because guard >= 2*r and max(|x|+|y|) on the radius-r disk is sqrt(2)*r. Both curves are convex. Their cardinal and half-edge endpoints occur in the same cyclic order; the straight connector segments therefore cannot cross, and lie outside the disk except at their circle endpoints. Thus each sector boundary is a Jordan curve. Positive Jacobians, exact whole-face gluing, the validated G18 exterior partition, and the one-to-one outer and bore boundary cycles give degree one and global coverage/injectivity of this fixed template. This proof does not cover arbitrary partial intersections or other charts.
