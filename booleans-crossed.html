Geometry and analysis checks

The independent oracle compares the rational charts with de Boor evaluation of both operands at 5,145 points per result. It also compares volumes at Gauss orders 12 and 20 against the reference volumes within 10⁻⁶ mm³. Changing the rise shifts the transverse interval vertically while preserving its height and the overlap volume in these cases.
