Audit a fixed section family against independent profiles and topology.

The four curved templates are qualified only for these frozen profiles: their planar contours are simple and nested without overlap, and their positive planar Jacobian certificates preserve orientation. The radial profile differs from its ideal circular arc by at most ARC_ERROR_MM; the E6 arc’s endpoint/tangent construction and this bound preserve the contour ordering. Sector interiors therefore tile the disk/annulus once (topological degree one), while the disjoint square/core regions fill the remaining stated domains. Extrusion over disjoint z intervals preserves that injective tiling. This is a template-specific transfer argument, not a certificate for arbitrary blocks or arbitrary perturbed profiles.
