Expand description

A manufactured axial extension solution on an extrusion-like solid: u=(0,0,sin(pi*(z-z0)/L)). It is zero on both axial ends, is driven by the analytic body force and receives exact Cauchy traction on all design faces. (lambda+2*mu)*(pi/L)^2*sin(pi*(z-z0)/L) in z.
