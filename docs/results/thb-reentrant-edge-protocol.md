# THB approximation at a reentrant edge: frozen protocol

## Starting point and scope

- Starting revision: `1c63c1280e1bef16fcf9a533b6fe70d6e0a8d158`.
- Affected files: new `src/thb_reentrant_edge.rs`, `tests/thb_reentrant_edge.rs`, an independent oracle and fixture under `tests/references/`, and this report. No existing geometry, field, or mechanics result is changed.
- Geometry: the L section `[-1,1]^2 \\ [0,1]^2`, represented by three unit-square affine charts, with the physical reentrant vertex at `(0,0)` and opening angle `3π/2`. Each chart is extruded by the exact constant interval `z∈[0,1]`. The section solve is the scalar antiplane/Poisson problem; it is not a full 3D elasticity solve.
- Exact field: `u(r,θ)=r^(2/3) sin(2θ/3)` for `0≤θ≤3π/2`, where `θ` is measured clockwise from the positive x-axis. It is harmonic in the open section and has the expected `r^(-1/3)` gradient singularity at the reentrant vertex. Prescribe its exact trace on the outer L boundary. At chart interfaces require matching scalar traces. The corner is measure zero; evaluation there is excluded from pointwise comparisons.
- Baseline: tensor-product cubic B-splines, open at the boundary and simple interior knots (C2), with an 8×8 prepared span grid per chart. Refinement is dyadic and nested. The local hierarchy refines nested corner boxes of four spans per side, touching the reentrant vertex, with identical tangential trace partitions on both incident charts. This is a small, explicit THB profile; it is not a general multipatch adaptive basis.

## Preconditions, comparisons, and thresholds

1. All three affine charts cover the L section exactly, have positive orientation, and meet on the two declared full edges only. The seam maps are identical up to the declared chart orientation.
2. Marking boxes are closed-domain subsets and may touch the physical boundary. Every refinement level is nested, and the reentrant-corner boxes agree under the seam maps. Empty, nonnested, out-of-domain, or seam-incompatible marks are refused.
3. Compare uniform levels 0–3 (8, 16, 32, 64 spans/side) with multilevel THB of the same maximum depths. Report active scalar DOFs and full/uniform tensor comparison DOFs separately. Compare the error-versus-DOF curves and report equal-level uniform error as an upper-quality reference: because the uniform space contains the THB space at the same finest level, local error is not expected to be strictly lower there. A successful efficiency result requires at least one THB level to have lower H1 error than a uniform level using at least 20% more active scalar DOFs. Otherwise retain the measurements and mark the efficiency criterion failed.
4. Exact transfer acceptance: rational refinement/truncation matrices reproduce constants, have full expected rank, and reconstruct every active cell's Bernstein coefficients exactly. Across both chart interfaces the complete trace polynomials agree exactly on every common subinterval. The global constant axial extrusion is exact and separate from section scalar DOFs.
5. Independent oracle: a separately coded Python `Fraction`/Cox–de Boor implementation reconstructs the 1D knot refinement and THB active/truncated basis, checks partition of unity and polynomial trace identities, and compares sampled basis values/gradients with Rust exports. It does not call the Rust builder.
6. Error integration uses tensor Gauss-Legendre order 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 on every section leaf. The cells incident to the singular vertex use a Duffy map from the reference square to the triangle sector, so the origin is not sampled and the radial singularity is integrated with a singularity-aware rule. Accept a reported level only when consecutive relative energy-error changes are at most `2e-3` and L2-error changes at most `5e-4`; otherwise report it as unresolved. The exact solution, gradient, and normalization are fixed above.
7. The study succeeds only if all mandatory levels are built, exact transfer and oracle checks pass, and the quadrature acceptance is met. The THB-versus-uniform efficiency criterion is the error-versus-DOF condition in item 3; no same-level error advantage is required. If the measured curve misses that condition, retain the results and report failure without changing boxes, thresholds, or the solution.

## Frozen refinement schedule and exclusions

The prepared level 0 has 8×8 spans per chart. Uniform controls use levels 0–3 (8, 16, 32, and 64 spans/side). THB uses the fixed nested marked boxes `[0,1/2]^2`, `[0,1/4]^2`, and `[0,1/8]^2` in each chart, giving the same maximum level 3 at the singular vertex. Each box is four cells wide per side in its own parent level. The fixed checked allocation cap is 128,000 active functions. Box widths are fixed before measurements.

The protocol excludes full 3D elasticity qualification, arbitrary boundary conditions, general cross-patch refinement, automatic error estimators, and claims of optimal convergence. Exact solution values on the far boundary are non-polynomial; their lift is projected onto the exact THB/uniform trace space with the same independently checked boundary mass projection for each discretization. Projection error and interior approximation error are reported separately.
