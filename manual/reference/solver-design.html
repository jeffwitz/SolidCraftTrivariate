Adaptive solves, pressure and design derivatives

Marking and transfer

An adaptive Q2 leaf carries three contributions to its squared indicator: the volume residual, the traction jump on interior faces and the natural boundary residual. The data structure also records whether an error upper bound is available. Here it is false. The value depends on the current mesh, loads and discrete solution.

For a fraction \(0<\theta\leq1\), AdaptiveResidual::bulk_mark sorts the squared indicators in descending order and selects the shortest prefix containing at least that fraction of their sum. Leaf identifiers order ties. A vanishing indicator selects no leaves. Dörfler proved convergence for an adaptive Poisson algorithm. The kernel uses its marking rule in elasticity. Error reduction on an elastic part must be measured separately. Dörfler (1996).

The constrained multilevel solver assembles a coarse operator \(P^T K P\) using the nested Q2 transfer. It factors the coarsest operator by Cholesky and adds an inverse reduced-diagonal correction at each finer level. All levels use the same homogeneous mounting. An unsupported mount or an inconsistent transfer fails during construction. For timings, include preconditioner setup, iterations and assembly in addition to the residual. For the linear iteration underlying the solver, see Hestenes and Stiefel (1952).

Nearly incompressible materials

As Poisson’s ratio approaches one half, a displacement discretization can overconstrain the volume strain. The optional projected_bulk operator projects the Q2 displacement divergence onto an element-local Q1 pressure space with eight coefficients. It condenses the pressure variables before the global solve. Its recovery operator returns coefficients of mean stress with tension positive, in MPa. Compare reaction equilibrium, the true 3D residual, and a displacement or stress observable against a reference under the same loading. Elguedj, Bazilevs, Calo and Hughes derive small- and finite-strain projection methods for higher-order NURBS. The kernel’s qualified law in this module is small-strain isotropic elasticity. Author manuscript.

Design derivatives

differentiate-job MODEL JOB DIRECTION OUTPUT holds the chart connectivity and quadrature rule fixed while differentiating geometry, material, loads and supported constraint equations. For a reduced equilibrium \(K_r q=f_r\), the tangent satisfies \(K_r q'=f'_r-K'_r q\). For one scalar observable the adjoint uses the transpose of the same reduced operator. Moving prescribed values and trace coefficients enter through the derivative of the lift. Record both objective derivatives and replay the central difference inside the same topological regime. A change of active features calls for a new regime. Wall, Frenzel and Cyron describe shape sensitivities with NURBS design variables in isogeometric structural optimisation. Wall et al. (2008).
